# Settings and Branding

Your organisation's settings cover branding, company information, timezone,
and your own password. See [Company Branding](company-branding.md).

## Where to find it

Tenant workspace → **Settings** (in the sidebar footer profile menu, or via
Settings in the main menu).

## In this section

- [Company Branding](company-branding.md)

## Timezone

Every organisation has a timezone, set by an administrator, which is used
throughout the platform — for example, to decide what counts as "today" when
evaluating payment deadlines, and what time of day deadline reminder emails
go out.

Under **Your Timezone**, you can optionally set your own timezone if it's
different from your organisation's. This only changes how dates and times are
*displayed* to you (for example, the time shown on a scheduled meeting) — it
does not change any deadline or reminder timing, which always follows the
organisation's timezone. Leave **Use company timezone** ticked to simply see
everything in your organisation's timezone.

!!! note
    Platform-wide configuration is managed separately by SureSign
    administrators and is not covered in this guide.
