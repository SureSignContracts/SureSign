# Company Branding

## Where to find it

Tenant workspace → **Settings**, which has five tabs: **Company Branding**,
**Branding Preview**, **Company Information**, **My Preferences**, and
**Change Password**.

## Company Branding tab

- **Company Logo** and **Cover/Banner Image**
- **Display Name**, **Company Description**, **Tagline**
- **Custom URL** (on eligible plans — see below)
- **Accent Colour**
- **Email Footer / Signature**
- **Letterhead Header** and **Letterhead Footer** images (used on generated
  PDF documents)

## Custom URL

On eligible plans, you can choose a branded SureSign address (e.g.
`your-company.suresigncontracts.app`) for the links your customers click in
appointment and consultation emails, instead of the default SureSign
address.

- **Choosing a URL** — enter a name using lowercase letters, numbers, and
  hyphens. We'll check it's available and show you a preview before you save.
- **Changing it** — you can change your URL at any time. Links already sent
  to customers under your previous address keep working — nothing breaks.
- **Removing it** — you can remove your custom URL at any time; your
  customer-facing links go back to the default SureSign address.
- **Not seeing this option?** It's included on our Professional and
  Enterprise plans — contact your account manager if you'd like to add it to
  your plan.
- This is separate from a fully custom domain you own (e.g.
  `contracts.yourcompany.com`) — that's arranged directly with our team
  today, not self-service.
- If you have a Custom URL set up, visiting it now shows a branded sign-in
  page with your logo and company name before handing off to the regular
  SureSign sign-in form — your team never enters a password anywhere but
  the regular sign-in page itself.

## Branding Preview tab

See how your logo, accent colour, and company name will look across four
places customers actually see them — your **Workspace**, the branded
**Login** page, a **Public page** (like an appointment or consultation
booking, including its loading and error states), and **Email**
notifications. Switch between Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile widths to check
each. Changes here update instantly as you edit the Company Branding tab —
nothing is saved until you click **Save Changes** there.

## Company Information tab

- Contact Email, Contact Number, Website
- Street Address, City, State/County, Postcode, Country
- VAT/Tax Number
- **Organisation Timezone** — applies to every user in your organisation
  unless they set their own override under My Preferences

## My Preferences tab

- **Your Timezone** — leave **Use company timezone** ticked to see everything
  in your organisation's timezone, or untick it to pick your own. This only
  changes how times are displayed to you; it never changes any deadline or
  reminder timing. See [Settings Overview](overview.md) for more on how
  timezone display works.

## Change Password tab

- Current password, new password, confirm new password.

## First-time setup

If your organisation is new to SureSign, most of these fields are first
collected during the [onboarding wizard](../getting-started/first-day-checklist.md)
when the first user signs in — you can revisit and change any of it here at
any time.

## What happens after you save

Each tab saves independently. Branding changes (logo, accent colour,
letterhead) apply immediately across your organisation's projects, dashboards,
and generated documents.
