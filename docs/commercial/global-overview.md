# Global Commercial

## What this is

An organisation-wide view of your commercial position across every project
you can access: company cash position, deadlines at risk, work awaiting
action, and a per-project roll-up. It answers "how healthy is our commercial
position, and what needs attention across all projects," rather than the
detail of any single project.

Global Commercial is **read-only**. It monitors and prioritises; it does not
replace project-level commercial work. You cannot create or edit a payment
application, certify, issue a notice, or edit a variation from this page.
Every actionable item links directly to its project record.

## Who can use it

Client users see the commercial position across their own organisation's
projects. Super Admin and Admin see the same monitoring view (following the
same access rules as [Reports](../reports/overview.md)).

## Where to find it

Select **Commercial** from the main sidebar (outside of any project).

## What you will see

- **Company Cash Position**: Certified to Date, Paid to Date, Outstanding,
  and Retention Held, totalled across all your accessible projects. If your
  organisation has projects in more than one currency, you will see one
  complete block per currency rather than a single combined figure. Amounts
  in different currencies are never added together.
- **Deadlines at Risk**: Payment Notice deadlines, Pay Less Notice
  deadlines, and Final Dates for Payment across all projects, grouped as
  Overdue, Due Today, Due Soon (within 7 days), and Upcoming. Each item links
  straight to the payment application it belongs to.
- **Awaiting Action**: payment applications awaiting submission, awaiting
  certification, or certified but unpaid; variations awaiting valuation or
  awaiting a decision. Only work that needs attention is shown, not a full
  list of every record.
- **Per-Project Commercial Position**: one row per project, with contract
  value, certified, paid, outstanding, retention, pending/approved variation
  value, and a count of items requiring attention. Select a row to open that
  project's Commercial area.

## How totals are calculated

Global Commercial uses the same certified/paid/retention/variation
calculations as [Reports](../reports/overview.md), so the figures on both
pages are always consistent with each other, because they are computed by
the same underlying logic rather than calculated separately.
