# AI Limitations

Understanding what AI analysis does not do is as important as knowing what it
does.

- **AI results are suggestions, not decisions.** Extracted information must be
  reviewed and confirmed by a person before SureSign treats it as current data
  for a contract or trade package.
- **AI does not make legal or contractual determinations.** It cannot tell you
  whether a clause is enforceable, favourable, or compliant with the underlying
  contract form — it summarises and extracts what is written in the document.
- **Quality depends on the source document.** A clean, text-based PDF or Word
  document gives better results than a poor-quality scan.
- **One analysis at a time per record.** You cannot run two analyses
  simultaneously on the same contract or trade package.
- **Hourly limits apply.** There is a limit on how many analyses you can start
  per hour; exceeding it means waiting before trying again.
- **Cancelling a running analysis may still be billed.** If you cancel an
  analysis after it has already started processing, SureSign tells you the
  in-progress usage may still be charged — cancelling before it starts
  processing is free.
- **Confirmed data can be overwritten by a later confirmed analysis**, not
  automatically merged with it — always check the overwrite warning before
  confirming a new analysis over an existing one.
- **The general AI Assistant chat (Tools menu) is not yet available.** It is
  unrelated to contract/subcontract analysis and does not affect it either way.
