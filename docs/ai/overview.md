# AI in SureSign

SureSign uses AI analysis in two places, both opt-in and controlled by your
organisation administrator:

1. **Contract AI analysis** — reads an uploaded contract document and extracts
   key terms, dates, and obligations. See [Contract Analysis](contract-analysis.md).
2. **Subcontract AI analysis** — the same idea, applied to a trade package's
   subcontract document. See [Subcontract Analysis](subcontract-analysis.md).

!!! note "AI Assistant"
    The **AI Assistant** chat page (Tools menu) is not yet available. The two
    AI features described above are the only ones currently working.

!!! important "AI output is a suggestion, not a decision"
    Everywhere SureSign uses AI, the output is an extracted or generated
    suggestion for a person to review. It only becomes part of your project's
    authoritative data once you [confirm it](../contracts/confirming-analysis.md).
    SureSign does not treat AI output as a legal or contractual determination.

## Turning AI on or off

AI features are switched on or off for your organisation by an administrator.
If AI is off, the analyse actions on contracts and trade packages will tell
you AI features are disabled — contact your organisation administrator if you
need this enabled.
