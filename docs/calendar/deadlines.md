# Deadlines

Deadlines that appear on the Calendar are calculated from the modules that own
them:

- Payment due date, final date for payment, payment notice deadline, and pay
  less notice deadline come from [Payment Applications](../commercial/payment-applications.md).
- Milestone dates come from [Programme](../programme/overview.md).
- Delivery document due/expiry dates come from
  [Delivery Documents](../delivery-documents/overview.md).
- Risk review dates come from [Risks](../risks/overview.md).

Use the **Overdue** quick filter on the Calendar to see everything currently
past its deadline across the project.
