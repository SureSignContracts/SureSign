# Calendar

## What this is

A single view of every dated item across a project: contract dates, payment
deadlines, notices, programme milestones, and final account dates.

## Who can use it

All roles can view the calendar for projects they have access to.

## Where to find it

Project → **Calendar**.

## Event types

Commencement, Completion, Key Date, Obligation, Payment Due, Final Payment, Pay
Less Notice, Payment Notice, Milestone, and Final Account — each shown with its
own colour in the legend.

## Views

- **Month** — a Gantt-style grid with a "Today" button and previous/next
  navigation.
- **Week**
- **Agenda** — a list view grouped by date (used automatically on smaller
  screens).

Your chosen view is remembered the next time you open the calendar.

## Filters

Quick filters: All, Overdue, Today, This Week, Upcoming. Additional dropdown
filters for Category, Priority, and Status.

## Using the calendar

- Select a day to open a panel listing that day's events.
- Select an event to open its detail: module, category, priority, status, due
  date, any linked contract/trade package, and a description. Use **View in
  {Module}** to jump straight to the source record.
- The sidebar also shows an Operational Summary: Overdue, Today, Next 7 Days,
  and Next 30 Days, each expandable.
