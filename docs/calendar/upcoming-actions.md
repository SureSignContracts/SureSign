# Upcoming Actions

The Calendar's **Operational Summary** sidebar groups what needs attention into
Overdue, Today, Next 7 Days, and Next 30 Days. Expand any section to see the
individual items, and select one to open its detail and jump to the source
record.

This is the most direct way to answer "what do I need to do next on this
project" without checking every module individually.
