# Reports

## What this is

A cross-project view for reviewing performance over a reporting period,
comparing periods, and exporting figures to present to directors, clients,
funders, or auditors. This is distinct from [Global Commercial](../commercial/global-overview.md),
which shows live, right-now operational status. Reports answers "what
happened during this reporting period, and how can I present or export it";
Global Commercial answers "what needs my attention right now." The two are
never merged.

## Where to find it

**Reports** in the sidebar.

## What you'll see

- Summary tiles: Total Contract Value, Certified to Date, Outstanding Balance,
  and Open RFIs, all totalled across every project you have access to (live,
  all-time figures, unaffected by the reporting period below).
- **Commercial Summary**: select **View** to open the full Commercial
  Summary Report:
    - A **reporting period** selector: Today, Last 7 Days, This Month, Last
      Month, Quarter, Year, or a Custom Range.
    - Report metadata: organisation, reporting period, generated date and
      time, effective timezone, who generated it, currency context, and
      report type.
    - An **Executive Summary**: a short automatically generated paragraph
      built from the real calculated figures below (never fabricated).
    - **Financial Position**: Certified to Date, Paid to Date, and
      Outstanding for the selected period.
    - **Retention Position**: Retention Held.
    - **Commercial Pipeline**: counts and values of applications awaiting
      submission, awaiting certification, and certified but unpaid.
    - **Variation Position**: Approved Variation Value and Pending
      Variation Value.
    - **Per Project Summary**: one row per project (read-only, with no
      editing actions here; open the project's own Commercial area for that).
    - **Export to PDF** or **Export to Excel**: downloads the same report
      with the same metadata, section structure, and currency grouping shown
      on screen.

If your organisation has projects in more than one currency, the report shows
one complete set of sections per currency rather than a single combined
figure. Amounts in different currencies are never added together.

Other report types (Project Status, RFI Summary, Document Register, Financial
Forecast) are listed but marked **Coming soon**. They are not available yet.
