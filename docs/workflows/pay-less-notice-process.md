# Pay Less Notice Process

## Purpose

Formally state that you intend to pay less than the notified sum, and why.

## Prerequisites

An existing payment application (and usually a payment notice already issued
against it).

## Role required

Super Admin or Admin.

## Steps

1. Open the payment application.
2. Select **Issue Pay Less Notice**.
3. Confirm the notice date and notified sum (pre-filled from the latest
   payment notice where one exists).
4. Enter the total deductions and the grounds/basis for them.
5. Enter who is issuing it, and confirm.

## Expected result

The application's status becomes **PLN Issued**, and a Pay Less Notice PDF is
generated automatically.

## Warning

!!! warning
    SureSign will let you issue a pay less notice after its calculated
    deadline has passed — it does not block a late notice. Late notices can
    have serious contractual consequences under your contract, so check your
    contract's own requirements before issuing, especially if the calendar
    shows the deadline as already overdue.

## Common mistakes

- Issuing without clear grounds recorded — record the basis for the deduction
  clearly, since this is a formal notice.

## What to do next

Continue the application through to certification if not already done, and
watch the [Calendar](../calendar/overview.md) for the final date for payment.
