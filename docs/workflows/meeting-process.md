# Meeting Process

## Purpose

Record a project meeting's agenda, minutes, attendees, and action items.

## Role required

Super Admin or Admin.

## Steps

1. [Create the meeting](../meetings/creating-meetings.md) — title, type, date,
   location, attendees, agenda.
2. Add the minutes and action items as they are finalised.
3. Set the status to Issued once the minutes are ready to share, and Approved
   once signed off.

## Expected result

A complete meeting record with minutes and action items, viewable by everyone
with access to the project.

## Linked modules

- [Meetings](../meetings/overview.md)
- [RFIs](../rfis/overview.md) — RFIs are often raised from meeting actions

## Common mistakes

- Leaving a meeting in Draft indefinitely — issue it once minutes are agreed
  so others can rely on it.

## What to do next

Follow up on action items in the relevant module (for example raising an RFI
or variation if a meeting action requires it).
