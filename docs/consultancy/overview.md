# Consultancy

SureSign Consultancy connects you with an experienced construction
professional for a private consultation — not an AI chatbot, a real person.
Use it to talk through a payment application, a notice, a variation, an
extension of time, or any general contract administration question for your
project.

**Consultancy is not legal representation, dispute resolution, or
adjudication services.** If your matter has escalated into a formal dispute,
see [SureSign Adjudication](https://suresigncontracts.app/adjudication).

## Booking a consultation

### As a SureSign customer

1. Go to **Consultancy** in the sidebar.
2. Select **Book a Consultation**.
3. Choose a consultation type, a date and time, and your timezone.
4. Describe your enquiry — a short title and description, plus optional
   project stage, contract form (for example NEC, JCT, FIDIC), and preferred
   outcome. This helps the consultant prepare.
5. Submit. You'll receive an email confirming your consultation, with a
   calendar invite attached.

Your consultations are listed under **Consultancy**, where you can view the
details of any consultation or cancel one that hasn't happened yet.

### Without a SureSign account

Visit the [Consultancy page](https://suresigncontracts.app/consultancy) on
the SureSign website. Choose a consultation, pick a time, and complete the
short enquiry form — no account required. Your confirmation email includes
a link you can use to cancel or reschedule, if you booked in time for that
to still be available, and a link to a page where you can check your
consultation's status, join by Google Meet when it's time, and download a
calendar invite — all without creating an account.

## Available consultations

The consultations on offer (name, duration, and price) are configured by
SureSign and may change — the booking page always shows the current list.
A short **Quick Consultation** is typically available as a low-cost
introduction to Consultancy, intended to help you decide whether a longer,
paid consultation would be useful — it does not include a detailed document
review or a written report.

## What to expect

- If your consultation is held over a video call, you'll receive an email
  once the Google Meet link is ready, with a "Join Google Meet" button —
  you can also join from your consultation's detail page (or, without an
  account, the link in your confirmation email).
- The consultant will discuss your enquiry during the time you booked.
- Your consultation's status (for example confirmed, completed, or
  cancelled) is shown on its detail page and updates automatically as it
  progresses — you don't need to do anything to move it along.
- Once your consultation is complete, you'll receive a short email
  thanking you and letting you know a written summary is being prepared.
- After the consultation, if your consultant writes up a summary, it will
  appear on your consultation's detail page and you'll receive an email
  with a secure link to view it — without an account, that link opens a
  dedicated summary page; no sign-in required. Not every consultation gets
  a written summary — a short Quick Consultation, for example, typically
  won't.
- If your consultation relates to a specific project, your consultant may
  link it to that project internally for their own reference. This is
  something your consultant manages, not something you need to set up
  yourself.
- Consultancy does not currently support uploading documents ahead of a
  consultation — this is on the roadmap.
