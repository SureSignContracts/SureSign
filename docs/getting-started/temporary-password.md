# Changing a Temporary Password

## What this is

When a Super Admin invites you, sets a temporary password for you, or forces a
password reset on your account, SureSign requires you to set a new password
before you can use anything else in the platform.

## Who this applies to

Any user whose account has been marked by a Super Admin as needing a password
change. This is not something you switch on yourself; it is set on your account
by an administrator, or automatically when your account is first created.

## What you will see

Instead of your normal dashboard or project workspace, SureSign shows a **Change
your password** screen. You cannot navigate anywhere else in SureSign until you
either complete this step or sign out.

## How to change your password

1. Enter a new password.
2. Confirm the new password.
3. Select **Update password**.

Once your password is updated, you are taken into SureSign as normal.

!!! important "You cannot skip this step"
    There is no "remind me later" option. The only alternative to changing your
    password is **Sign out instead**, which returns you to the sign-in screen
    without changing anything.

!!! note "Password requirements"
    Your new password must be at least 8 characters and include a mix of upper
    and lower case letters, numbers, and symbols.

## If an administrator gave you a one-time password

If a Super Admin used **Set Temporary Password** on your account, they will have
given you a specific password to sign in with. Use that password to sign in,
then follow the steps above to set your own password.
