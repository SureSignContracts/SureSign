# First-Day Checklist

Use this checklist the first time you sign in to SureSign.

1. **Sign in** with the email and password (or temporary password) you were
   given. See [Signing in](signing-in.md).
2. **Change your temporary password**, if prompted. See
   [Changing a temporary password](temporary-password.md).
3. **Complete onboarding**, if your organisation is new to SureSign. If you are
   the first user of a new organisation, you will be guided through a short
   setup wizard covering your profile, your company details, and branding
   (logo and letterhead). You can update all of this later in Settings.
4. **Review your profile** under Settings — check your contact details are
   correct.
5. **Understand your role.** Read the [Roles](../roles/overview.md) page so you
   know what you can and cannot do.
6. **Open your dashboard** and review the summary tiles and recent projects.
   See [Dashboard](../dashboard/overview.md).
7. **Open an assigned project** and review its Overview page.
8. **Check the project's contract**, if one has been uploaded, and see whether
   AI analysis results are waiting for review. See
   [Contracts](../contracts/overview.md).
9. **Check for notifications**, if your account has access to the notification
   bell. Otherwise, check a project's Overview activity feed instead.
10. **Find the Documents area** for your project or organisation, so you know
    where uploaded and generated files live.
11. **Identify your next action.** Look at the project Calendar or a module's
    status filters (for example, open RFIs or draft payment applications) to see
    what is outstanding.

!!! tip
    The **Help** page (in the main menu) lets you replay the guided product tour
    at any time if you want a refresher.
