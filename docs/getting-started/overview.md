# Getting Started

This section covers everything you need before you start using SureSign day to
day: signing in, understanding the dashboard, and finding your way around.

## What you need before you start

- An email invitation from your organisation's Super Admin or Admin, or a login
  provided to you directly.
- A temporary password (if your account was created by an administrator) or your
  own chosen password.
- To know your role: **Super Admin**, **Admin**, or **Client**. See
  [Roles](../roles/overview.md) for what each can do.

## The dashboard, in brief

After signing in, most users land on their **dashboard**, which shows a summary
of active projects, open RFIs, pending variations, documents added this month,
and payment applications awaiting action, along with your most recent projects
and open RFIs. Administrator accounts are taken to a separate dashboard focused
on account and organisation management. See [Dashboard](../dashboard/overview.md)
for full detail.

## Getting help

- The **Help** area (in the main menu) links back to this guide and lets you
  replay the guided product tour.
- If something looks wrong or is missing, check [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/index.md)
  before contacting support.
