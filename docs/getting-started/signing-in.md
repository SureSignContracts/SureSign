# Signing In

## Where to find it

The sign-in page is the first screen you see when you open SureSign and are not
already signed in.

## What you need

- Your email address.
- Your password (the one you chose when you accepted your invitation or
  registered, or a temporary password if an administrator gave you one
  directly).

## How to sign in

1. Open SureSign in your browser.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Select **Sign in**.

If your credentials are correct, you are taken to your dashboard (or, for Super
Admin and Admin accounts, the admin dashboard).

## If your details are wrong

If your email or password is not recognised, SureSign shows **"Invalid
credentials."** and does not tell you which of the two is wrong. Double-check
both and try again. See [Troubleshooting: Unable to sign in](../troubleshooting/sign-in.md)
if you are still stuck.

## If your account cannot sign in

You may see one of the following messages instead of reaching your dashboard:

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Your account has been deactivated." | A Super Admin has switched off your account. | Contact your organisation's Super Admin. |
| "Your account has been banned." | A Super Admin has banned your account (a reason is recorded internally). | Contact your organisation's Super Admin. |
| "You must change your password before continuing." | An administrator has required you to set a new password. | See [Changing a temporary password](temporary-password.md). |

## Forgotten password

Use the **Forgot password** link on the sign-in page to request a reset email.
Follow the link in the email to set a new password.

!!! note
    If you do not receive the reset email, check [Troubleshooting: Sign-in problems](../troubleshooting/sign-in.md)
    before requesting another one.

## Verifying your email

If you registered yourself, SureSign sends a verification email, and
following the link confirms your email address. If you were invited by a
Super Admin or Admin, accepting your invitation confirms your email
automatically, since opening a secure, personal invitation link already
proves you control that mailbox — no separate verification email is sent.

## Signing out

Use **Sign out** from the user menu (available from the profile icon in the
sidebar footer) to end your session. If an administrator revokes your sessions
or deactivates or bans your account, you are signed out automatically the next
time SureSign checks your account, and you will need to sign in again once the
issue is resolved.
