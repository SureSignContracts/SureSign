# Navigating SureSign

SureSign has two distinct areas depending on your role: the **tenant workspace**
(for day-to-day project work) and a separate platform administration area for
accounts with broader administrative access.

## Tenant workspace (all roles)

The left-hand sidebar is organised into:

- **Dashboard** — your summary view.
- **Workspace** — Projects, Commercial (cross-project view), Site Admin,
  Documents (organisation-wide library).
- **Tools** — Reports, AI Assistant, Team, Help.

The sidebar footer has a profile menu with **Settings**, a light/dark theme
toggle, and **Sign out**.

!!! note "No notification bell in the tenant workspace"
    The notification bell is not currently shown in the tenant workspace. If
    you are a Client user, check the **Overview** page of a project or your
    **Reports** for recent activity instead.

## Project workspace

Opening a project shows a project-specific sidebar with:

- **Overview** — project summary and activity feed.
- **Contract group** — Contracts, Commercial, Variations, Notices, Programme,
  Delay & EOT, Risk Register.
- **Communications group** — RFIs, Meetings.
- **Delivery group** — QA Reports, Snagging, Site Reports, Delivery Documents,
  Closeout.
- **Disputes group** — Adjudication (if enabled for your organisation).
- **Documents** and **Calendar** (always shown at the bottom of the project menu).

Trade packages (subcontract work packages) are opened from within a project and
have their own workspace with tabs: Overview, Commercial, Programme, Delay &
EOT, Compliance, Documents, AI Analysis, and Activity.

## Platform administration

Accounts with broader administrative access sign in to a separate
administration area rather than the tenant dashboard. Platform administration
is not covered in this guide — if you need an administrative action performed,
contact your organisation's administrator.

Project detail pages and your own **Settings** are shared between the tenant
workspace and the administration area, so administrator accounts can still
open and work inside a project the same way a Client user would.

## Common interface patterns

- **Status pills** — coloured badges showing the current state of a record (for
  example Draft, Submitted, Certified). See [Status Reference](../reference/statuses.md)
  for what each one means per module.
- **Create / Edit modals** — most records are created and edited in a pop-up
  form rather than a separate page.
- **Tabs** — used within Commercial, trade package workspaces, and Delay & EOT
  to group related record types.
- **Filters and search** — most list pages offer a search box and one or more
  status/category filters above the table.
- **Toast messages** — brief confirmation or error messages appear at the corner
  of the screen after an action (for example "Contract created" or "Upload
  failed. Please try again.").

## If a page or project will not open

If you try to open a project (or another record) that does not exist, or that
you do not have access to, SureSign shows the same "not found" style page
either way. This is deliberate: it does not tell you whether the record exists
elsewhere. If you believe you should have access, contact your organisation's
administrator.
